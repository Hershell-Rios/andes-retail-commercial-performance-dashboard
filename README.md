# 📊 Andes Retail Group | Commercial Performance Dashboard (2024–2025)

Business Intelligence dashboard developed in **Power BI** to analyze commercial performance across countries, customer segments, and product categories.

The project transforms transactional sales data into actionable business insights, enabling executives to monitor revenue trends, profitability, and sales performance through an interactive dashboard.

---

# 📌 Business Context

Retail companies generate large volumes of transactional data every day. However, without an effective reporting solution, identifying sales trends, profitable customer segments, and product performance becomes difficult.

This project addresses that challenge by creating an executive dashboard that consolidates key business metrics and supports strategic decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Objective

Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard capable of answering key business questions such as:

- How did total revenue evolve between 2024 and 2025?
- Which customer segments generate the highest revenue and profitability?
- Which product categories contribute the most to the business?
- How does commercial performance vary by country?
- What are the main commercial KPIs that executives should monitor?

---

# 🔍 Process / Methodology

The project followed a complete Business Intelligence workflow.

### 1. Data Preparation

- Imported and validated data in Power BI
- Corrected data types
- Cleaned inconsistent records
- Created calculated columns
- Created business KPIs using DAX

---

### 2. Data Modeling

Built a structured data model optimized for dashboard performance.

Main calculations included:

- Total Revenue
- Units Sold
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Year-over-Year Growth (YoY%)
- Revenue by Customer Segment
- Revenue by Product Category

---

### 3. Dashboard Design

The dashboard was designed following executive reporting best practices:

- Clear KPI cards
- Interactive filters
- Country comparison
- Segment comparison
- Category analysis
- Consistent color palette
- Executive layout

# 💡 Key Insights

### ✅ Premium customers generated the highest revenue

Premium customers contributed the largest share of total revenue and profitability, making them the most valuable customer segment.

---

### ✅ Peru showed the strongest revenue contribution

Country-level analysis revealed differences in commercial performance, with Peru contributing a significant share of overall revenue.

---

### ✅ Product categories contributed differently to business performance

Some product categories consistently generated higher revenue, helping identify opportunities for inventory and marketing optimization.

---

### ✅ Profitability remained relatively stable

Although revenue changed between years, profitability remained consistent across customer segments.

---

### ✅ Executive KPIs provide immediate business visibility

The dashboard enables decision-makers to monitor:

- Revenue
- Units Sold
- Profit
- Profit Margin
- YoY Growth

from a single executive view.
